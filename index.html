About

A little about me

I'm Muhammad Nihal, a third-year Computer Science Engineering student at Sri Shanmugha College of Engineering and Technology, affiliated with Anna University, Chennai. I'm expected to graduate in 2028.

My programming background started with C and C++, where I learned to think about memory, logic, and problem structure. I then moved into Java, which introduced me to object-oriented programming, and Python, which I use for scripting and exploring algorithms. More recently, I've been learning HTML, CSS, and JavaScript to understand how software meets the people who use it.

What drives me is the satisfaction of building something that works — something a real person can use. I'm interested in writing clean, understandable code and learning the thinking behind good software decisions, not just copying solutions.

I'm currently building this portfolio and three original projects to develop my skills, establish a public presence, and prepare for internship opportunities.
